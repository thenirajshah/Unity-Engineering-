# Unity Engineering V3 delivery

V3 rebuilds the website on the existing Python static generator, CSS, JavaScript and Cloudflare Pages setup. The main catalogue remains at 23 products across the six approved categories. Distillation Columns retain their own route and Autoclave Reactors stay under Reactors. The earlier site history and inbound redirect rules are preserved.

## Delivered

- Research inventory of 115 unique public websites. The report ranks 20 and records the priority ten and award evidence.
- Graphite and stainless-steel homepage, warm-paper catalogue, local fonts, revised company and capability pages and a shared design system.
- New reactor hero and inspection scene, 21 replacement product illustrations, six category thumbnails, 23 catalogue cards and 23 equipment detail crops.
- New engineering capabilities page, product navigation to technical requirements, gallery zoom, category and text search, equipment-specific WhatsApp links and email and WhatsApp quote drafts.
- Process and quality content framed by the design basis and agreed scope. Site and PDF share the equipment inventory and sizing data, including reactor working volume in kL.
- Native motion features and five existing opt-in interactive assembly viewers. The shell-and-tube model has tube-bundle and exploded modes.
- Updated social imagery, contextual internal linking and production-specific sitemap, canonical and robots behavior.

## Items requiring company evidence

- The product renders, process imagery and the existing 3D models remain conceptual. Photos verified as actual Unity Engineering equipment or facilities and approved mechanical models have not been supplied.
- Numeric capacities, ratings and in-house operations require documentary approval. The site publishes sizing units and enquiry fields, and keeps project-specific values as sizing on request.
- Competitor inventory is evidenced by live HTML and specific information architecture patterns. Individual visual and cross-device tests of all 115 third-party sites were outside the measured coverage.
- Performance measurements are synthetic. Field Core Web Vitals require production visitor data.

## Operations

The GitHub-connected Cloudflare Pages project publishes from `main` with the existing static build command and HTTPS origin. Production checks and release-specific benchmark results are recorded in the test report.
