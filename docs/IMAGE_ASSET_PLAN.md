# V3 equipment image strategy

The catalogue contains 23 approved machine subjects. Unity Engineering's logo is supplier-provided. Equipment and facility imagery is treated as conceptual unless a Unity Engineering photograph is explicitly documented. Competitor imagery and the supplied reference archive are excluded from public delivery.

Review subjects against the approved product title, principle, principal silhouette, supports, process connections and drive or access assembly. Check the equipment fit in both cards and mobile detail pages. Keep a stronger existing concept when a proposed replacement introduces doubtful geometry. Represent material appearance as satin stainless steel without assigning a verified grade to an illustration.

For accepted new studio concepts, export optimized WebP images at their native cropped resolution plus a smaller responsive version. Reframe the hero for portrait screens to preserve the equipment silhouette. Use a full-frame zoom and a clearly named component crop from the same confirmed composition where feasible. Source and verification status belong in `ASSETS.csv`. Record final replacements and limitations in the V3 release report.

## Image briefs

- **Reactor hero** — large three-quarter limpet coil reactor with dished end, support feet, legible half-pipe turns and a mounted agitator. Workshop composition, graphite left for text, steel on the right, warmer practical light. Keep a safe portrait crop.
- **Reactors and containment** — GMP reactor with high-leg hygienic configuration, external-coil reactor, horizontal high-pressure vessel on saddles and hinged autoclave. Distinct drive and closure arrangements.
- **Thermal** — horizontal shell-and-tube assembly with removable head, box exchanger with bolted rectangular casing, kettle reboiler with enlarged vapour shell and tall distillation column with no central drive.
- **Batch equipment** — ANFD with agitator and filter bottom, non-agitated Nutsche, ribbon blender with a U-trough and helices, fluid-bed dryer with separate product bowl and exhaust filter head.
- **Inspection editorial** — machined tube sheet, coherent bore geometry and optional gloved handling. Concept demonstration of an activity.

## Release inventory

- 21 product primaries refreshed after side-by-side review of the proposed concept renders and the existing catalogue.
- The existing Reboiler and Rotocone Vacuum Dryer (RCVD) concepts had stronger full-equipment framing and adequate source resolution. Both received new catalogue and detail crops.
- All 23 products have a full-equipment view, a component crop from that image and a framed catalogue version. Six category thumbnails avoid downloading the detail images in category navigation.
- The hero has two desktop delivery sizes and a dedicated mobile crop. The quality feature has a new inspection concept.
- Newly generated sheet cells deliver at up to 619 pixels per side. The hero is 1536 pixels wide. The original generation sheets stay out of the public site and repository.

The visual review identified plausible equipment categories and recognizable assemblies. It does not verify manufacturing drawings, model geometry, materials, pressure ratings or configurations. No company-photo or drawing approval was supplied for this release.
