## Method and limits

The per-site inventory records the opening source heading or title, routes for product discovery and enquiries, media and motion signals, mobile viewport metadata, search metadata, HTML transfer size and request time. A heading is evidence of content hierarchy. Media tags indicate assets declared in the returned HTML. A library name is a source signal and can originate in a bundle or description. HTML request time was captured from one network location and is not a browser performance benchmark. HTML size excludes fetched CSS, scripts, images and video.

Read the first labels as homepage composition and navigation evidence. A missing route or heading is a limitation of the sampled HTML. Typography, photography craft, touch behavior, layout, menus, hover, transitions and runtime-only WebGL cannot be graded reliably from source. The ranked evaluation includes observable content and navigation patterns. It does not claim a cross-device visual or Lighthouse test for every reference.

## Design decisions

- **Identity and typography** use the supplied orange logo, a graphite and paper palette, architectural rules and large display type. Apple and Fratelli Simonetti informed the hierarchy and sequence without supplying brand elements.
- **Equipment photography** gives the hero a dedicated, high-quality reactor visual. Each product keeps a distinct engineering subject and clearly labelled concept imagery.
- **Catalogue and technical credibility** put filtering, sizing units, principle of operation and quote links on high-traffic paths. De Dietrich, Alfa Laval, GEA and Buchiglas informed this structure.
- **Navigation and lead generation** keep industry and equipment entry points visible on the homepage. Product-specific quote and WhatsApp paths stay under the customer’s control.
- **3D and motion** use the interactive work as reference for focus, timing and progressive enhancement. Preserve a native scroll, local assets and optional model loading.
